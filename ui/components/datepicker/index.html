DatePicker

Behaviour

What the date picker does for you.

Behaviour

Paging

The arrows move a month; the month and year menus jump.

Keyboard

Days, arrows and menus are buttons: Tab reaches them, Enter or Space uses them.

Screen readers

Each day is named by its date, like August 14, 1991, and the picked one reports aria-pressed. The arrows are named Previous Month and Next Month.
