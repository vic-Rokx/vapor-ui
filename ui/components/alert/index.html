Fields

Set them after init to change how it looks and what it does.

Alert

content

?*const fn (*Alert) void

= null

Draws what's in the box: text, buttons, anything. Your buttons call close to dismiss it.

closed

bool

= true

Whether the alert is hidden. open and close set it.

background

Color

= .palette(.background)

The box's background.

border

Border

= .round(.palette(.border_color_light), .all(12))

The box's border and corners.

label

[]const u8

= "Alert"

Names it for screen readers: what it asks about.

trigger

Vapor.Element

= .{}

Bind your open button to it with .ref(&alert.trigger), and the focus goes back there when the alert closes.

on_enter

?*const fn (*Alert) void

= null

Called when Enter is pressed while the alert is open. The alert stays open; close it here if the action should.

on_close

?*const fn () void

= null

Called when it is dismissed: Escape, or a click outside. Your own buttons' close calls don't call it.

Behaviour

What the alert does for you.

Behaviour

Closing

Escape, a click outside the box, or your call to close.

Keyboard

Opening it moves the focus into it; closing gives the focus back to its trigger. While open, Escape closes it and Enter calls on_enter.

Screen readers

The box has role=alertdialog, aria-modal and its label. The backdrop is hidden from them and can't be tabbed to.

Backdrop

The page behind is blurred and can't be clicked; the box sits in the middle.

Animation

Fades and grows in, and fades and shrinks out, in 200ms.
