Behaviour

What the sidebar does for you.

Behaviour

Clicking

An item is marked current and on_item_click is called; the sidebar doesn't navigate by itself.

Keyboard

Headings and items are buttons: Tab reaches them, Enter or Space uses them. A closed group's items are skipped.

Screen readers

Headings and the menu button report aria-expanded; the current item reports aria-pressed. A closed group is hidden.
