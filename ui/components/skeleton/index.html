What skeleton does for you.

Behaviour

Pulse

It fades in and out, and holds still when the system asks for less motion.

Screen readers

They skip it. Mark the region that loads aria-busy, as the second example does.

Startup

Opaque.new() registers the pulse; call Skeleton.new() instead if you do not call that.
